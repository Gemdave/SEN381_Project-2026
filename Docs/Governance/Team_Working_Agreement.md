## Core Principles & Code of Conduct
- **Psychological Safety & Open Communication:** Ideas, technical concerns, and mistakes will be discussed constructively without personal fault.
- **Collective Ownership:** While specific roles own distinct domains, every member is accountable for the overall health and quality of the system baseline.
- **Professional Reliability:** Commitments made during team planning are binding. If a task is blocked, the owner must notify the team immediately via WhatsApp/Teams rather than waiting for scheduled meetings.
