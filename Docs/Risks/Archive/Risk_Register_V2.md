## Method  
**Likelihood (P)** and **Impact (I)** use a three-point scale: L = Low (1), M = Medium (2), H = High (3). **Score** = P x I.  

| Score | Priority |
|---|---|
| 9 | Critical |
| 6 | High |
| 3 to 4 | Medium |
| 1 to 2 | Low |

Ratings are **inherent** (before the listed mitigation takes effect). **Status** is a single value: Open, Mitigating, Mitigated (control in place and evidenced), Realized, or Closed.
